Series Subtitles

Choose which subtitles a video starts with, per library or per series. A series rule wins over the rule of its library. Changes are saved immediately.

Library rules

Applies to every video of a library, including the ones added later. Example: Anime in French, Movies with no subtitles.

Series rules

Tick one or several series, pick the subtitles, then add the rule.

Off (recommended): a rule sets the default, and subtitles can still be changed or turned off for one playback. On: the rule is always applied when playback starts.
